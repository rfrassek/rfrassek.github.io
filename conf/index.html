Workshop on integrable systems

Date: 22. - 23. June 2026

The goal of the workshop is to gather researchers from the Po Valley (

🌺
), their nearest and next-to-nearest neighbors, in order to foster discussions on topics related to integrable systems.

The topics include: String and gauge theories, Probability, Quantum physics, Statistical physics, Condensed matter, Quantum groups and Representation theory.
