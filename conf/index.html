All around Integrability II

Bologna, 22–23 June 2026

Workshop on Integrable Systems

The goal of the workshop is to gather researchers from the Po Valley, their nearest and next-to-nearest neighbors, in order to foster discussions on topics related to integrable systems.

The topics include:

  • String and gauge theories
  • Probability
  • Quantum physics
  • Statistical physics
  • Condensed matter
  • Quantum groups
  • Representation theory

Previous edition: Modena 2025

Mini-courses by

  • Marco Rossi (Università della Calabria)
  • Niels Benedikter (Università di Milano)

Speakers

  • Nicolò Brizio (Università degli Studi di Padova)
  • Adam Chalabi (Università di Torino)
  • Giacomo Gori (CNR)
